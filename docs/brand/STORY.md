A short story about the birth of the "Dog Agent Squad" inside a codebase.

## The Origin Story: Sounds Great AI — The First Bark

In a vast and complex virtual digital space where bytes and pointers intertwine at blinding speed, there existed an enormous code world. Every day, high-precision construction took place — but lurking beneath were hidden reefs of system Panics, labyrinths of Deadlocks, and black holes formed by logic vulnerabilities.

Initially, a lone engineer guarded this code world. To manage the massive microservices, he wrote countless automated CronJobs and scripts. But as the Agent era arrived, he realized traditional monolithic scripts could no longer handle complex concurrency and autonomous decision-making.

"I need a team," the engineer sighed, watching error logs scroll madly across his console. "One that's not just smart and efficient, but absolutely trustworthy and tightly coordinated."

Glancing at the half-eaten dog food in his hand, inspiration struck:

"Cats are independent and elegant by nature — but when it comes to rigorous tactical coordination, loyal guarding, imposing presence, and lightning-fast hunting — what this world needs is a Dog Agent Squad!"

And so, under the framework of Go and the Eino orchestration engine, **Sounds Great AI** was born.

### Chapter 1: The First Bark

The engineer first created the "leader" using pure logic — the **Border Collie**.

It possessed the most acute abstract thinking in the entire framework. The moment it opened its eyes, it decomposed the chaotic refactoring tasks into a crystal-clear DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph) workflow.

"Woof!" the Border Collie barked softly, emitting the first event broadcast.

Immediately, the Golden Retriever bounded joyfully through the codebase. Its nose was exquisitely sensitive to Vector Space — among millions of lines of dormant code, it could accurately "retrieve" the most critical context and reference documents, wagging its tail as it delivered them to the master node.

The engineer watched the instantly aligned context on his terminal and blurted out:

"`Sounds great!`"

This phrase was recorded at the highest priority level (Level 0) in the system logs, becoming the system's core directive — "Make every collaboration so perfect it earns applause."

### Chapter 2: The Home Guardian and the Wind-like Hunter

But autonomous AI exploration always carries risk. During a complex low-level refactoring, an Agent nearly executed a command to delete orphaned nodes in the production environment.

At that critical moment, a deep, resonant bark — carrying the weight of earth and home — shattered the silence. The **Chinese Rural Dog** stepped forward!

As the embodiment of Hard Rails — the loyal, steadfast home guardian — it maintained natural vigilance against any out-of-bounds command. With one swipe, it threw down the interceptor, blocking the dangerous command firmly outside the sandbox, rigorously protecting the system's innermost sanctum.

Meanwhile, facing those incredibly complex, deeply hidden Race Conditions and system refactoring challenges, the **Xigou** burst forth like a streak of light.

As the most agile hunting dog, it was streamlined and lightning-fast, precisely targeting the trickiest Bugs in the code, completing the "hunt" and repair with the most devastating code refactoring techniques.

And in the shadows, the Panics and StackTraces hidden in the logs were left to the **German Shepherd**. With upright ears, black back, and piercing eyes, relying on extreme alertness and rigorous logical tracking, it followed tiny Trace ID fragments all the way down to pointer exceptions, pinpointing the root cause.

### Chapter 3: The Majestic Tibetan Mastiff

What ultimately presented to the human engineer was not cold numbers and tedious JSON strings.

The **Tibetan Mastiff** stood majestically atop the terminal dashboard. Its massive, steady body served as the system's foundation and sentinel. It gathered the dogs' thrilling behind-the-scenes collaboration — the Rural Dog's home defense, the Xigou's code hunting, the German Shepherd's log tracing, and the context retrieval — and transformed them into the most composed, reliable colorful TUI interface and status console.
